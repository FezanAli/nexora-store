# NEXORA Design Direction

## Personality
Premium, technical, minimal, confident. The interface should feel closer to a high-end product launch than a generic marketplace template.

## Visual system
- Background: warm off-white with deep ink surfaces
- Accent: electric lime for high-intent actions and small highlights
- Secondary accent: cool violet/blue gradients in product storytelling blocks
- Radius: 18–30px for large surfaces, 12–16px for controls/cards
- Borders: quiet low-contrast borders rather than heavy shadows
- Motion: subtle lift, scale and glow — never excessive

## Typography
Use a clean grotesk/system stack initially. For production, consider a licensed/web-safe geometric sans and keep body copy highly readable.

## Homepage rhythm
Announcement → Header → Hero → Categories → Trending → AI shopping assistant → Deals → Trust → Footer.

## Differentiator
The site should not look like Amazon/Best Buy. Product discovery is editorial: fewer, stronger modules with bold typography, spacious composition and intelligent guidance.
